Shipping & delivery policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

  • Where we deliver — homes and offices in and around Tiruchirappalli within our delivery radius of the store. The app checks your address before you order.
  • When — the expected delivery date is shown at checkout and on every order, and you can follow each step in the app.
  • Charges — the delivery charge depends on your distance from the store and is shown in the cart and at checkout before you pay. Orders above the free-delivery amount shown in the app are delivered free.
  • Minimum order — if the store sets a minimum order value, the app shows it in the cart.
  • At delivery — please check your items. For cash-on-delivery orders, pay the delivery person in cash or by UPI.
  • Delays — if we can’t deliver on the expected date (for example in heavy rain), we will contact you.

We deliver only within India, from our store in Tiruchirappalli.
