Jeya Staff app

For Jeya Hyper Store staff — orders, product photos and store admin

Android phones

Download for Android

Checking the latest version…

  1. Tap Download for Android and open the downloaded file.
  2. If the phone asks, allow Install unknown apps for Chrome (or your browser), then go back.
  3. Tap Install, open Jeya Staff and sign in with the username your store admin gave you.

When a new version comes out, the app shows Update — tap it to download it from here.

iPhone

Use the web app — it does everything the Android app does:

  1. Open staff.jeyahyperstore.in in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Jeya Staff from the home screen and sign in.

Computer or tablet? Just use staff.jeyahyperstore.in.
